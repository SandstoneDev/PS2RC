# Takedown policy

PS2RC is a generic recompiler. The project distributes no game data and no game
builds: each player converts their own legally obtained copy of a game on their own
PC.

If you hold rights in material you believe appears in this repository, or in a
release or page of the project, without permission, contact the maintainer:

- open an issue on GitHub,
- write on the community server ([discord.gg/wV8x46ZTDu](https://discord.gg/wV8x46ZTDu)), or
- send an e-mail to sandstonedevpsp@gmail.com.

Name the material (file, commit or page) and say why it should be removed.

On a takedown or removal notice, the maintainer removes the named material promptly
and does not publish it again: no re-uploads and no mirrors.

GitHub's DMCA process applies as usual; a contact beforehand is welcome but not
required.
